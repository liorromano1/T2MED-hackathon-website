PulseAI — Real-time sepsis
detection at the bedside

Team PulseAI built a lightweight clinical decision support tool that monitors continuous vital signs and lab data streams to flag early sepsis onset — up to 6 hours before standard protocols would detect it. Deployed as a pilot in two hospital wards within three months of winning.
